Jacky

A team-owned AI agent, currently in internal testing.

Jacky is a shared assistant a team connects its own tools to (Notion, Google Drive, GitHub, Slack). It only reads what the team explicitly shares with it, and works on the team's behalf inside a shared chat thread.

This site exists to satisfy the OAuth sign-in requirements of the tools Jacky connects to while the product doesn't yet have its own domain.
