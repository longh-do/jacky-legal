Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Jacky is in early internal testing. This policy describes what the product actually does today; it will be replaced by a fuller policy before Jacky is offered to anyone outside its own team.

What Jacky is

Jacky is a shared, team-owned AI agent. A team connects it to tools they already use — Notion, Google Drive, GitHub, and Slack — and Jacky works inside a shared chat thread that every team member can see and steer.

What data Jacky accesses

Jacky only reads what a team member has explicitly connected it to: specific Notion pages, a specific Google Drive folder, specific GitHub repositories, or specific Slack channels. It does not read anything a team hasn't shared with it, and it does not access a person's private messages, files, or accounts unless that person explicitly brings that content into a shared thread themselves.

What Jacky does with that data

Jacky uses the content it's given to answer questions and produce work (documents, summaries, and similar artifacts) inside the team's shared threads, at the team's direction. Messages, connected content, and the artifacts Jacky produces are stored so the team's thread history stays available and so Jacky can pick up work across sessions.

Who can see it

Only members of the team that owns a given thread can see that thread, Jacky's connections, and what Jacky produces in it.

Third parties involved

Jacky's underlying language model is provided by Anthropic. Application hosting and storage run on Cloudflare. Connections to Notion, Google, GitHub, and Slack are brokered through Composio. None of these providers receive more than the specific content a team has connected Jacky to, used only to operate Jacky for that team.

What Jacky does not do

Jacky does not sell data, does not use connected content to train models, and does not share one team's data with another team.
